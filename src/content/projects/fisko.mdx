Fisko is a product I build with Robert Beres. It is for Romanian accountants,
who still spend a large part of every month typing receipts and invoices into
their accounting software by hand.

The software most small firms here run on is SAGA or WinMENTOR. Fisko sits in
front of it. The accountant drops in a month of documents, the AI reads them,
the accountant checks each one, and Fisko writes a single import file. Nothing
is retyped.

## Who did what

We split it cleanly. I built the application: the review screen, the receipt
detection and cropping, the extraction prompts and schema, and the SAGA,
WinMENTOR and CSV exports, plus the bilingual landing page. Robert built the
platform under it: the AWS infrastructure in Terraform, CI/CD, sign-in and
usage quotas, Stripe billing, and the account area for exporting or deleting
your data.

## How it works

The extraction runs on AWS Lambda and asks Gemini for a strict JSON schema
rather than free text, at a low temperature, so every document comes back with
the same sixteen fields or not at all. The prompt is written as a Romanian
accountant working from the national chart of accounts, and when it isn't sure
of an expense account it falls back to the suspense account instead of
guessing.

Receipt detection is classical computer vision, not a model: a Python service
on Lambda runs OpenCV edge detection at several sensitivities, keeps the shapes
that look like receipts, and merges the overlaps.

The exports were the part that needed the most care. An import file that SAGA
or WinMENTOR rejects is worse than no file at all, so each format follows the
vendor's documented structure, escapes every special character, and never
leaves a numeric field empty. The WinMENTOR format was checked against a real
installation.

## Where it stands

Fisko is live at fisko.ro, with a free trial of five documents and monthly
plans. It is early. There are no customer numbers to quote yet, and suggestions
tailored to each client's own history are the next thing to build.
